# Abu Dhabi / Zandvoort / Baku — source and accuracy notice (0.16)

Research: 8 November 2026. These are **modern playable layouts for all five car selections**, not historical road reconstructions, scans or photorealistic replicas. Source/photo record: `expansion-references.json`.

## Retained geographic data

- Centreline: Tomislav Bacinger, MIT `f1-circuits.geojson`, IDs `ae-2009`, `nl-1948`, `az-2016`. The compact original features are in `expansion-map-source.json`; original license in `CIRCUITS-LICENSE.md`. Corner-preserving Chaikin smoothing (one pass, 0.20 weight; Baku 0.10), local equirectangular metres, approximately 5 m resampling. Official length metadata differs slightly from the rendered line.
- © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Bounded retained way geometries/tags are in `expansion-map-source.json`; the W Hotel supplement is in `expansion-hotel-source.json`. Source timestamps are retained. Core API bounded map fallback was used for Zandvoort after Overpass errors. Hotel building-part outlines and connecting bridge were obtained from the core OSM API, not inferred from a generated image.
- Castle corridor masonry uses mapped thin building-way **1502989778** (three mapped levels), corroborated by the castle reference; it is not the separately tagged palace `city_wall` way 299450006 hundreds of metres away.
- Current pit routes: Yas Marina ways 176695254 → 176695255 → 176695253; Zandvoort 38144527; Baku 1513267145. Coordinates projected/resampled; endpoints registered to the race road. Widths, box locations and heights are estimates. Yas's mapped pit tunnel is lowered by an authored 5 m with approximate ramps. This is not an engineering reconstruction of its tunnel portals.
- Present-day building ways within 300 m, outside the complete road plus 3.5 m, are retained; crossing footprints are rejected, not drawn through the road. Building heights use mapped height/levels or an explicitly labelled 9 m default. Clipping/exclusion is not disabled for landmarks. The hotel's separately modelled connecting walkway has an estimated 9 m minimum floor clearance above the local road.
- Geographic elevation: Copernicus GLO-30 DSM for Yas Marina and Zandvoort. GLO-30 is unavailable for Baku in the public bucket, so **GLO-90 (90 m) is explicitly used there**, not relabelled as 30 m. Bounded grids and exact COG URLs are retained in `elevation-dem.json`; license/attribution in `ELEVATION-NOTICE.md`. Surface models see roofs/vegetation, not surveyed road heights. Smoothed Baku Old City climb is approximate.
- Actual georeferenced 512 × 512 Sentinel-2 Level-2A true-colour crops (2025), under the existing Copernicus notice in `IMAGERY-NOTICE.md`, are included. `imagery.json` records source scene, date, URL, bounds and coarse vegetation mask. They guide context, not a stretched road texture. Desert brightness is not claimed to be cloud-free imagery. Water polygons are coarse original traces from those crops and exclude race/pit roads; they are not exact shorelines.

No stock-photo previews or third-party trackside photo pixels are redistributed in the game, repository, report or ZIPs. Local research thumbnails outside the repository are not release assets. Landmark geometry and textures are original code.

## Important limits

- Constant authored road widths: Yas 14 m, Zandvoort 12 m, Baku 8 m. **Baku's actual straights are much wider than its castle section**; a fully variable-width surveyed corridor is not implemented. Roadside appearance, bypass widths, castle wall height/tower spacing, hotel lattice, facades, barriers and runoff extents remain estimates.
- Zandvoort banking changes the same road/kerb mesh, wheel support heights and car body roll in Rust/web/native. Cross-slope gravity is represented through heading-relative grade, but this is not a full 3D tyre/suspension model or calibrated banked-corner dynamics. Raised apron edges use simplified earth retaining faces rather than surveyed dune transitions.
- Temporary viewing stands without a mapped footprint have `osm_id: 0` and are explicitly photo-guided authored placements. No invented OSM IDs or historical facility claims.
- Existing ten track/layout/surface records are preserved. New scenes remain modern for the 1967/1988/2004 cars too, with in-game substitute labels.
- Sponsor signs, exact grandstand seating inventories, individual palms, live time-of-day lighting and architectural interiors are not exact replicas. All car models remain original shared-base approximations, not scans.

## Offline regeneration

`python3 scripts/build-expansion.py` rebuilds the three geographic/context records from retained sources. `python3 scripts/build-elevation.py`, `python3 scripts/build-corner-details.py` and `python3 scripts/build-reference-scenes.py` rebuild shared derived geometry without network access. Only explicitly named `import-*` tools fetch sources. Network reimports are optional and may change OSM snapshots; do not overwrite a validated release silently.
