# Car comparison reference photographs

Ten photographs by **Lukas Raich**, from the **2021 Austrian Grand Prix**, are retained as resized 960 × 540 JPEGs in `data/car-reference-images/`. They were obtained through Wikimedia Commons' imageinfo API. Exact file-page URLs, original-image URLs, author, license, changes and SHA256 checksums appear in `data/car-references.json`.

All ten are licensed **Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The resized/recompressed photographic adaptations are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license, without additional restrictions. Credit: **Lukas Raich / Wikimedia Commons**. Source links are also displayed beside every image in `car-comparison.html`. Photographs were resized and JPEG-recompressed only; they were not retouched or used as car textures.

The comparison HTML combines these separately licensed photographs with original in-engine showroom captures. The original game code/geometry and rendered screenshots remain separate from the photographic works; brand names and reconstructed sponsor marks do not imply endorsement or corporate licensing. Original car atlases contain **no reference-photo pixels**.

Game captures use the real production `referenceCar` meshes/materials, including team variants; the HTML does not substitute photographs or generated concept art for game renders. The side-view cameras are standardized but not calibrated to each photographer's focal length. No pixel-registration or numerical visual-accuracy score is claimed. Geometry, small logo placements and sponsor typography remain approximate. No verified chassis scans were added (0/57 season entries).
