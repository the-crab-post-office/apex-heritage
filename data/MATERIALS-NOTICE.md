Downloaded 8 November 2026 from `https://ambientcg.com/get?file=ASSET_1K-JPG.zip`. Retained only 1K (1024 px long edge; some sources are rectangular) colour, OpenGL normal and available roughness maps (Gravel001 has no supplied roughness map and uses a roughness value of 1), recompressed at JPEG quality 85 to keep the game lean. No ZIPs, previews, displacement maps, unused alternate normal formats or larger resolutions are kept. Maps repeat at metre scale; increasing the tiling detail is not claimed as higher-resolution real circuit imagery.

The six window/facade families (stucco/shutters, red brick, glass curtain wall, stone, industrial and balcony hotel) are original canvas composites using these CC0 bases, not photographs of the corresponding real buildings. Car shapes, wings, radiator grilles, rims and markings are original authored geometry without official team/sponsor logos. The supplied Monaco photograph is a reference only and is not redistributed as a texture.

Sentinel-2 data remains the coarse vegetation-placement guide; its low-resolution aerial JPEGs are no longer drawn on the ground. There is no claim that these tile materials survey the exact real asphalt, buildings or vegetation at each circuit.
