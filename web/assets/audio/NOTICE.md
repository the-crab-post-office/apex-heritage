Changes by APEX / HERITAGE: trimmed low/high-load passages, 85 Hz high-pass, converted to 24 kHz mono signed 16-bit little-endian PCM, DC removal, normalization, and 80 ms tail/head crossfades. Playback transposes and blends continuously with engine RPM, with additional original synthesized sound layers. Exact source windows and authored playback RPM anchors are in `recordings.json`; source download URLs and credits are retained there. These are real **trackside pull-away recordings**, not an isolated multi-RPM studio bank, and not exact replicas of the game's 1967/1988/2004 cars. The Toleman Hart source is a turbo four-cylinder, not the 1988 V6. Ambient sound and source RPM variation cannot be completely removed. The 2023 preset is still procedural: no verified matching modern V6 recording was bundled. The 1962/1983/1996 references are not mislabeled as matching the selected roster year.
