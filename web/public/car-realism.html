2026 / compact chassis

User references 1, 2, 3 and 7: short-wheelbase proportions, shouldered body, thinner static wing profiles, recessed ducts and larger covered rims. FIA concept paint is not assigned across the teams.

2023 / slim sidepods

User references 4, 5, 6 and 8: separate sculpted ground-effect body, slim early-Mercedes contour, downwash shoulders, spoon-wing forms and rim covers. This is a representative early configuration, not every later update.

2021 / smaller open rims

User references 9 and 10: retain the distinct 2021 narrow-nose, squared wings, open-spoke wheels, bargeboards and flat-floor character; new compound surfaces, duct depth and material separation do not turn it into a later-era car.

What this comparison does not prove. These are still real-time original approximations, not photorealistic replicas, scans, constructor CAD or calibrated photogrammetry. Shared era bases have estimated team contour adjustments; small aerodynamic details and sponsor letterforms remain simplified. User references include concept renders, launch views and particular race liveries. The supplied year assignments are respected, but a concept is not presented as exact constructor engineering. Lighting and stage changed, so pixel differences are not a quantitative geometry score. Original user photographs are private references and are not redistributed on this page.
Skin/cap winding, monotone surfaces, recessed radiator throats, thinner chord-scaled wings, matching pod/decal deformation, carbon/rubber micro-surfaces and satin covers are implemented in the actual models. 1967/1988/2004 model arrays and the handling/contact/track sources remain unchanged.
