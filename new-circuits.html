Three new circuits.
Brakes that bite.

Abu Dhabi, Zandvoort and Baku: mapped modern layouts, photographic research and original shared scenery. Six real production captures below—not concept art, AI-generated venue images or a claim of surveyed accuracy.

Current building / ground follow-up: all 13 venues now have explicit venue/photo-guided facade assignments across 4,419 mapped building parts, with conservative roof/height estimates and safe road cutbacks. Yas hotel shell spacing and connector detail are refined. This is not 4,419 individually photo-matched replicas. Zandvoort T3 is gentler (10°); T14 retains 18° with a retimed blend. Continuous bank-following earth and a lower closure bed replace the old vertical gravel curtains. Sources and accuracy limits.

Brake priority, not pedal cancellation.

Full braking now suppresses engine thrust even if the accelerator remains held. Brake force no longer dwindles to 20% at the cornering limit: braking keeps its force without cutting the steering budget. Combined-input rear slip is still modelled; this is not a strict friction-circle simulation. Aero-supported load improves high-speed braking. Half-pedal and wet-road stops remain progressively longer.

Full accelerator plus full brake produces the same stop as full brake alone. Old lap records remain stored but use a fresh handling namespace.

Scope: modern layouts for all five car eras, explicitly labelled substitutes. Existing ten circuit/layout/surface records remain unchanged. New Zandvoort banking is part of the road/kerb geometry and wheel support, not a visual-only tilted backdrop. No stock-photo pixels are redistributed.
